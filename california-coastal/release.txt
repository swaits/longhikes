Published copy: line.geojson is the file's nine section tracks (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8a, 8b) in file order, as 13 LineStrings (32,475 points): section 4 is cut at Tomales Bay (a 5.0 km straight line across the water), section 6 at Morro Bay (0.4 km), section 8a at the Balboa Island ferry (0.3 km) and section 8b at the Coronado ferry (four points drawn over the bay are dropped). alternates.geojson is 29 of the file's other 34 tracks (the hiker's optional and side routes), as 29 LineStrings (3,071 points). Left out: the tracks the file labels Old trail, impassible; Impassible: detoured; Closed: Camp Pendleton Section; Beach option (packraft); and one of two copies of the Old Coast Road. The file's 508 waypoints are not used. Geometry only (longitude and latitude, no elevation, time, name or other metadata); coordinates rounded to 6 decimal places and written as compact JSON.
