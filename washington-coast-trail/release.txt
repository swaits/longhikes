Published copy: line.geojson is the file's 12 daily tracks (Day 1 to Day 12) in the order of the days, as 12 LineStrings (3,100 points); the file lists them out of order, and Day 5 is stored from Taholah to Moclips and is reversed here so that the whole track runs south to north. Nothing is drawn between the days: the bay crossings and bus rides are gaps. alternates.geojson is the file's three alternate tracks (La Push alternate, Overland Route Option, Two Man Rock overland), as 3 LineStrings (110 points). Left out: the three tracks that are bus rides (Day 6 bus 1, Day 06 bus 2, Day 7 bus) and the two tracks named Quinault North and Quinault South, which the itinerary page does not describe as walked (it has a bus from Taholah to south of Queets). The file's 110 waypoints are not used. Geometry only (longitude and latitude, no elevation, time, name or other metadata); coordinates rounded to 6 decimal places and written as compact JSON.
