outdoor.json, the outdoor map style of the trail pages, is derived from the
Liberty style of OpenFreeMap (https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles),
read 2026-11-15. Liberty is a fork of OSM Liberty
(https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty), which is a fork of OSM Bright
(https://github.com/openmaptiles/osm-bright-gl-style), derived from Mapbox Open
Styles. The notices of each follow.

The map design (look and feel) of OpenMapTiles styles is under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
It is credited in the corner of every map as "© OpenMapTiles", linked to
https://www.openmaptiles.org/, beside "© OpenStreetMap" for the map data.

The elevation, relief and contour parts (the olt-dem and olt-contours sources
and the hillshade and contour layers) and the colours are this site's own.
